# Pocket card

One page. The test is whether you can reconstruct it from memory at a whiteboard. If you can't, the session didn't transfer.

---

## Define it — seven questions

1. **Know** — What am I given? What is withheld?
2. **Restate** — Say it formally. Now say it by analogy.
3. **Divide** — Which parts separate and can be worked alone?
4. **Reduce** — Move a constraint until it's a problem I can already solve. *Which constraint did I move?*
5. **Analogize** — What solved problem does this resemble? What actually transfers — the mechanism, or only the vocabulary?
6. **Experiment** — What does each outcome rule out? A probe that rules nothing out is a guess.
7. **Plan** — First move, and how I'll know it worked.

---

## Sort the constraints — three bins

Ask of each: **who imposed it**, and **what happens if it's violated**.

- **Wall** — physics, arithmetic, law. Not priced. Rarer than it looks.
- **Price** — budget, schedule, compatibility, appetite. Violable at a cost. *Name the number.*
- **Assumed** — nobody can say who imposed it. This is where the freedom is.

---

## Classify it — four types

- **Known method** → execute, don't strategize.
- **No known method** → the full repertoire below.
- **Ill-defined** → stop. Go back to the seven questions.
- **Adversarial or reflexive** → the target moves when you do. Shorten the budget.

When it fits two, take the more demanding one.

---

## Attack it — seven moves

- **Change representation** — what am I looking at this *in terms of*?
- **Find the invariant** — what doesn't change no matter what I do?
- **Relax a constraint** — solve the easy version, then bend it back.
- **Specialize or generalize** — smallest real case, or drop the awkward specific.
- **Bisect** — cut the space in half, test which half, repeat.
- **Invert** — how would I guarantee failure? Now avoid that.
- **Transfer an analogy** — does the *mechanism* map, or just the words?

---

## Two rules that do the most work

**State the abandon condition before you start.** Once you're inside a line you'll find reasons to keep going. Budget and stopping observation, written down first.

**Write down what you ruled out.** The record of dead ends is the only part that compounds.
